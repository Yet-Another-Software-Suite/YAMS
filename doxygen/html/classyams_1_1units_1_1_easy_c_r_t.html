Detailed Description

Finds the absolute angle of a gear that turns more than once, from two absolute encoders on pinions with different tooth counts that both mesh with it.

Each encoder only knows its angle within one of its own rotations, but the two readings together are unique over GetUniqueRange(), which is lcm(encoderATeeth, encoderBTeeth) / sharedGearTeeth rotations of the shared gear.

Both encoders must be zeroed with the mechanism at zero, and both must read increasing angles when the mechanism moves in its positive direction. Set the offset and inversion on the encoders themselves, or adjust the readings before passing them in.

This is not a textbook Chinese Remainder Theorem solve. Every angle consistent with encoder A is tried, and the one whose predicted encoder B reading is closest to the real one wins, which stays stable with backlash and sensor noise. Created by team 6911.
