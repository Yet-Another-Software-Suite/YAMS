Detailed Description

Where a mechanism is on the robot, for each mechanism's GetPose3d(): where it is mounted, how it is turned there, the plane it moves in, and optionally the mechanism it is mounted on, such as an arm on an elevator's carriage.

MechanismPositionConfig position;
position.WithRelativePosition(wpi::math::Translation3d{0.2_m, wpi::units::meter_t{0}, 0.3_m}) // 20 cm forward, 30 cm up
.WithMovementPlane(MechanismPositionConfig::Plane::XZ);
