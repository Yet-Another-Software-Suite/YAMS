Detailed Description

DCMotor models of motors WPILib does not have a model of, like the Java YMotors.

This will be deleted once WPILib has a DCMotor of the FIRST A301. Use WPILib's then.

static constexpr wpi::math::DCMotor yams::motorcontrollers::YMotors::A301 ( int numMotors = 1)
inlinestaticconstexpr

The motor inside the FIRST A301, before its gearbox, at 12 V: 13,500 RPM free, and 0.078 N-m stalled, twice the 5.27 N-m at peak power of FIRST's 135:1 gearbox. FIRST does not give its currents, and the A301 is similar to a NEO 550, so it has the NEO 550's: 100 A stalled and 1.4 A free.

Every A301 has this motor. Its gearbox is part of the gearing given to the A301Wrapper's config.
