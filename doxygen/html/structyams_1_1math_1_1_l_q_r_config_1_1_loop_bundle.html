Detailed Description

template<int States>
struct yams::math::LQRConfig::LoopBundle< States >

A LinearSystemLoop with everything it points to. wpi::math::LinearSystemLoop keeps raw pointers to its LQR and Kalman filter, and wpi::math::KalmanFilter keeps a raw pointer to its plant, so all four live together here, on the heap, the plant first. Neither copyable nor movable, so the pointers never dangle.

Template Parameters
StatesNumber of modeled states: 1 for a flywheel, 2 for an arm or elevator.
