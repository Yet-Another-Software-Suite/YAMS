Detailed Description

A simulated Thrifty Nova driving a mechanism's SimSupplier. ThriftyLib's simulation ignores the Nova's closed loop, so this runs it as the Nova would, with the gains read back from the Nova: the voltage position and velocity loops with the PID slot's gains and its F times the target, and the motion profile with its slot's kS, kV, kA and kG. It keeps within the Nova's soft limits, and, with its absolute wrapping on, takes a position the short way, one mechanism rotation apart. Targets and gains are in mechanism rotations, as on a Nova with its sensor-to-mechanism ratio set, and the integral and derivative gains are per second, as the Nova's are. Open loop output, a duty cycle or a voltage, is passed on to the mechanism.

◆ UpdateSim()

void yams::motorcontrollers::simulation::NovaSimSupplier::UpdateSim ( )
overridevirtual

Advance the simulation by one loop iteration.

When the input has not been fed this loop (see FeedInput()), the motor duty cycle times the supply voltage is applied as the input and the battery voltage is updated. The physics are only stepped if the simulation has not already been updated this loop (see GetUpdatedSim()); stepping marks it updated, so a mechanism and its motor controller calling UpdateSim() in the same loop step the physics once.
