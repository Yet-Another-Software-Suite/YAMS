Detailed Description

Simulation of a motor whose leader's simulation has its motor too, such as an A301 following another, or a loosely coupled follower of a mechanism's motor controller.

It reads the leader's simulation, with its rotor values from its own gearing and its share of the current, and does not step a simulation of its own. Its current is in the leader's, so BatterySim does not count it again.

◆ UpdateSim()

void yams::motorcontrollers::simulation::FollowerSimSupplier::UpdateSim ( )
inlineoverridevirtual

Advance the simulation by one loop iteration.

When the input has not been fed this loop (see FeedInput()), the motor duty cycle times the supply voltage is applied as the input and the battery voltage is updated. The physics are only stepped if the simulation has not already been updated this loop (see GetUpdatedSim()); stepping marks it updated, so a mechanism and its motor controller calling UpdateSim() in the same loop step the physics once.
